04  What Happens Next

Three steps before anything is drawn.

01We call you back

Usually within a working day. We ask about the site, the scope and roughly what you want to spend — so nobody wastes a meeting.

02We visit the site

Measurements, light, access and services. For interiors we photograph everything; for new builds we check the plot and its rules.

03You get a proposal

Scope, stages, timeline and fee in writing. Once it is signed, Stage 01 starts and you meet the people doing the work.
