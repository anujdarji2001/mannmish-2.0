The Studio

Two disciplines,
one table.

Mannmish Design Studio was born from a shared vision: spaces that serve their purpose and still lift the people inside them. Our journey began with the union of two complementary skill sets — civil engineering precision and architectural creativity.

Slab · structure · envelope

01  Our Story

Great design sits where technical excellence meets artistic vision.

Every project we take on is approached with meticulous attention to detail, inventive problem-solving and a deep understanding of what our clients actually need — not just what they first ask for.

From residential homes to commercial spaces, we bring the same level of dedication to every project, so that each space we build tells a story of its own.

Because the drawings and the site sit under the same roof, decisions get made once. A detail that cannot be built never leaves the studio, and a change on site never quietly breaks the design.

Functional and beautiful. Sustainable and inventive. Timeless, yet of now.

04  How We Work

Five stages, from first conversation to keys.

Stage 01Brief & site study

We listen, measure and study the site — light, access, services and the rules that govern the plot — before a single line is drawn.

Stage 02Concept & planning

Massing, circulation and layout options, presented as drawings and 3D views you can actually read and react to.

Stage 03Design development

Materials, joinery, lighting and services are resolved together, so the detail you approve is the detail that gets built.

Stage 04Working drawings

Dimensioned drawings, schedules and BOQs for every trade — the set that keeps the site honest and the budget intact.

Stage 05Execution & handover

Vendor coordination, quality checks and site supervision through to snagging, cleaning and the day you move in.
